# Opportunity Engine App Constitution

## Mission

The Opportunity Engine exists to connect people who have valuable skills with businesses that have real problems but do not know where to find solutions.

The application discovers hidden business opportunities by identifying conversations where businesses express pain, frustration, inefficiency, or a need for improvement.

The goal is to create meaningful connections between problems and solutions.

---

## Core Product Philosophy

The application is not a job board.

The application is not a lead list.

The application is not a marketplace for spam.

The application is an opportunity discovery platform.

The platform finds problems first, then helps identify solutions.

---

## Primary Users

The platform is designed primarily for:

- Freelance developers
- AI developers
- Software engineers
- Automation specialists
- Consultants
- Independent professionals
- Small solution providers

The platform also benefits:

- Small business owners
- Entrepreneurs
- Decision makers

---

## Success Definition

The application succeeds when:

- Users discover opportunities they would not have found themselves.
- Users start meaningful conversations.
- Businesses receive helpful solutions.
- Users generate revenue from opportunities found through the platform.

Success is not measured by:

- Number of posts collected
- Number of sources connected
- Number of users alone

Success is measured by real outcomes.

---

## Quality Over Quantity

A small number of high-quality opportunities is more valuable than thousands of irrelevant posts.

The application must prioritize:

- Accuracy
- Relevance
- Intent
- Opportunity quality

Over:

- Volume
- Speed
- Number of results

---

## User Trust

The platform must always explain:

- Why a post was selected
- Why it matches the user
- What problem was detected
- What solution may apply

Users should never feel like the system is a black box.

---

## Anti-Spam Philosophy

The platform exists to create helpful conversations.

Users should not blindly pitch businesses.

The platform should encourage:

- Understanding the problem
- Providing value
- Asking questions
- Offering relevant solutions

---

## Feature Rule

Every feature must answer:

"Does this help users discover or win legitimate business opportunities?"

If not, the feature should not be prioritized.

---

## Future Expansion

Future features may include:

- More platforms
- Business profiles
- User profiles
- Messaging integrations
- Marketplace features
- Payments
- Advanced analytics
- AI sales assistance

Future expansion must never compromise the core mission.
