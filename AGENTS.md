# Custom Agent Rules for Opportunity Radar

## 1. Absolute Prohibition of Fake or Simulated Data
- **NO MOCK OR SIMULATED DATA**: Do not under any circumstances invent, generate, or display fake comments, simulated sub-threads, fictional discussion posters, or simulated forum upvotes.
- **AUTHENTIC SIGNALS ONLY**: The feed and detail cards must strictly display raw, authentic, unedited evidence/text captured directly from live scraped platforms (Reddit, Hacker News, etc.) or provided directly by real sources.
- **DIRECT PLATFORM LINKING**: Instead of simulating comments or native forum environments, provide clean, secure external links to let the user inspect the live discussions natively in their browser.

## 2. Opportunity Engine App Constitution

### Mission
The Opportunity Engine exists to connect people who have valuable skills with businesses that have real problems but do not know where to find solutions.
The application discovers hidden business opportunities by identifying conversations where businesses express pain, frustration, inefficiency, or a need for improvement.
The goal is to create meaningful connections between problems and solutions.

### Core Product Philosophy
- The application is not a job board, lead list, or a marketplace for spam.
- It is an opportunity discovery platform: it finds problems first, then helps identify solutions.

### Quality Over Quantity
- A small number of high-quality opportunities is more valuable than thousands of irrelevant posts.
- The application must prioritize Accuracy, Relevance, Intent, and Opportunity quality over Volume, Speed, or pure raw counts of results.

### User Trust
- The platform must always explain:
  - Why a post was selected
  - Why it matches the user
  - What problem was detected
  - What solution may apply
- Users should never feel like the system is a black box.

### Anti-Spam Philosophy
- Encourage understanding the problem, providing upfront value, asking questions, and offering highly tailored relevant solutions rather than blind pitching.

### Feature Rule
- Every feature must answer: "Does this help users discover or win legitimate business opportunities?" If not, it should not be prioritized.

## 3. Human Tone & One-Click Thought Leadership Guidelines
- **AUTHENTIC HUMAN VOICE**: All generated outreach, replies, and thought leadership posts must sound down-to-earth, natural, and conversational—written as if the founder is speaking casually off the cuff.
- **ZERO AI BUZZWORDS**: Strictly prohibit corporate jargon and AI clichés such as "game-changer", "delve", "synergy", "revolutionize", "leverage", "unleash", "cutting-edge", "supercharge", "seamless", "in today's fast-paced world".
- **ONE-CLICK THOUGHT LEADERSHIP POSTING**: P.A.C. proactively generates original, high-value value-add posts (for Reddit, LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook Groups, and Discourse) that demonstrate practical solutions to common operational bottlenecks. All generated posts must feature 1-Click review and posting actions.
- **PLATFORM-SPECIFIC ETIQUETTE**: Never spam or drop unsolicited promotional links in subreddits or forums. Offer genuine value first, build rapport, and allow commercial conversations to happen naturally.

## 4. Prime Directive & AI Agent Fleet Delivery Model
- **PRIME DIRECTIVE**: Landing Client #1 and Client #2 immediately to establish cash flow and prove the model.
- **AI AGENT FLEET DELIVERY**: All client projects are executed using a multi-agent model where specialized AI agents (OpenClaw, AI coding tools, n8n/Zapier automations, Python/JS scripts) do the building while the founder acts as Executive Lead Architect and Quality Director.
- **FEASIBILITY & SCOPE GUARDRAILS**: P.A.C. must only scope and pitch solutions that can be reliably built and deployed by our AI Agent Fleet under human supervision. Never sell impossible or unmaintainable scope.

## 5. Target Vertical Strategy (Client #1 & #2 Focus)
- **EXPLICIT REJECTION OF HEAVY CORPORATE & REGULATED HEALTHCARE**: Do not target hospitals, healthcare networks, enterprise medical clinics, or corporate enterprises for initial deals. They require HIPAA compliance sign-offs, procurement boards, vendor legal reviews, corporate liability insurance, and formal business licenses.
- **COMMERCIAL SOLVENCY & BUDGET QUALIFICATION**: Only target established micro-businesses generating active revenue (HVAC/plumbing contractors, active real estate brokers, boutique agencies with paying retainers). They have real cash flow, feel severe financial pain from operational bottlenecks, and easily afford $1,000–$3,000 solution fees. Strictly eliminate pre-revenue bootstrappers, students, or hobbyists with zero budget.
- **PRIORITIZE NIMBLE SOLO FOUNDERS & MICRO-BUSINESS OWNERS (1-10 employees)**: Target home service contractors (HVAC, plumbers, roofers, landscapers), real estate agents/investors, boutique marketing/recruiting agencies, local specialty shops, and independent consultants.
- **WHY**: The owner IS the decision maker. They experience immediate friction (lost leads, missed calls, manual CSV spreadsheet cleanup) and can approve a $500–$1,500 Stripe deposit on the spot in 5 minutes without needing a board approval, corporate license, or case study history.
